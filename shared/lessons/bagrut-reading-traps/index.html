Evidence in the text

ביסוס תשובה בטקסט
Use what the text says. A believable idea still needs evidence.

Evidence in the text · 01

The school installed water stations near the gym. Students can now refill their bottles without leaving the building.

Why did the stations make refilling easier?

Evidence & distractor check · 01

Why did the stations make refilling easier?

Evidence: “without leaving the building”

  1. A Students can refill inside the building.
    This preserves the evidence.
  2. B The water is healthier.
    Health benefits are not stated.
  3. C All students exercise daily.
    Daily exercise is not stated.
  4. D Plastic bottles are banned.
    No ban is mentioned.

Evidence in the text · 02

A library lends tools to local residents. Last month, thirty residents borrowed a drill. The report does not say how often they used it.

What does the report establish?

Evidence & distractor check · 02

What does the report establish?

Evidence: “thirty residents borrowed a drill”

  1. A The library sold thirty drills.
    The tools were lent, not sold.
  2. B Thirty residents borrowed a drill.
    This preserves the evidence.
  3. C Thirty residents repaired their homes.
    Borrowing does not prove a repair occurred.
  4. D Every borrower used the drill daily.
    Use frequency is explicitly unknown.

Evidence in the text · 03

The new app reminds users to take breaks. Its designers have not yet tested whether these reminders reduce stress.

What can we conclude?

Evidence & distractor check · 03

What can we conclude?

Evidence: “have not yet tested whether these reminders reduce stress”

  1. A The app is dangerous because screens cause stress.
    The emotional claim adds outside information.
  2. B The effect on stress is still untested.
    This preserves the evidence.
  3. C The app certainly reduces stress.
    A plausible benefit is not a tested result.
  4. D Users feel happier after every reminder.
    Happiness is not reported.

Evidence in the text · 04

The community garden received an award for involving teenagers in planning. Its vegetables were sold at a local market.

Why did the garden receive the award?

Evidence & distractor check · 04

Why did the garden receive the award?

Evidence: “for involving teenagers in planning”

  1. A It was the biggest garden in the city.
    No size comparison is given.
  2. B Teenagers helped plan the garden.
    This preserves the evidence.
  3. C Its vegetables tasted wonderful.
    Wonderful is attractive but unsupported.
  4. D The market donated all its profits.
    No donation is mentioned.

Read the exact task

קריאת השאלה וגבולותיה
Check the location, the scope and the number of answers required.

Read the exact task · 05

[1] Solar panels supply electricity to the museum. [2] Workshops teach visitors how to repair old clothes.

According to paragraph [2], what do visitors learn?

Evidence & distractor check · 05

According to paragraph [2], what do visitors learn?

Evidence: “Workshops teach visitors how to repair old clothes”

  1. A How to design a museum.
    Museum design is not mentioned.
  2. B How to repair old clothes.
    This preserves the evidence.
  3. C How the museum generates electricity.
    This belongs to paragraph 1.
  4. D How to install solar panels.
    Installation is not taught in either paragraph.

Read the exact task · 06

The article explains how city libraries are changing. It describes tool lending, study rooms and classes for adults. One class teaches bicycle repair.

What is the main topic?

Evidence & distractor check · 06

What is the main topic?

Evidence: “tool lending, study rooms and classes for adults”

  1. A A step-by-step guide to repairing bicycles.
    One example is narrower than the overall topic.
  2. B The cost of building study rooms.
    Construction cost is not discussed.
  3. C Why adults dislike reading.
    Dislike of reading is not stated.
  4. D The changing services of city libraries.
    This preserves the evidence.

Read the exact task · 07

The club offers free lessons to new members. Experienced members pay a small fee. All members may borrow equipment.

Who receives free lessons?

Evidence & distractor check · 07

Who receives free lessons?

Evidence: “free lessons to new members”

  1. A New members.
    This preserves the evidence.
  2. B All members.
    All broadens the group beyond the evidence.
  3. C Only experienced members.
    This reverses the groups.
  4. D People who borrow equipment.
    Borrowing does not determine lesson fees.

Which service is NOT offered?

Evidence: “It does not sell equipment”

  1. A Selling equipment.
    This preserves the evidence.
  2. B Lending cameras.
    This service is explicitly offered.
  3. C Teaching photo editing.
    This service is explicitly offered.
  4. D Displaying student photos.
    This service is explicitly offered.

BAGRUT READING WORKSHOP

Precision and strength

דיוק בניסוח ובחוזק הטענה
Keep small words, limits and certainty when paraphrasing.

Precision and strength · 09

Some students said the shorter lessons helped them concentrate. Others noticed no difference.

What did students report?

Evidence & distractor check · 09

What did students report?

Evidence: “Some students said the shorter lessons helped them concentrate”

  1. A No student noticed any change.
    Some reported improvement, so no is false.
  2. B Shorter lessons always prevent tiredness.
    Always and tiredness add stronger, different claims.
  3. C Shorter lessons helped some students concentrate.
    This preserves the evidence.
  4. D Every student concentrated better.
    Every replaces some.

איזה שינוי במסיח הפך אותו לשגוי? נסחו תשובה לפני המעבר.

Precision and strength · 10

The tickets are free only for children under twelve. Older visitors pay five dollars.

Who can enter without paying?

Evidence & distractor check · 10

Who can enter without paying?

Evidence: “only for children under twelve”

  1. A Children younger than twelve.
    This preserves the evidence.
  2. B All children, including sixteen-year-olds.
    The age limit excludes older children.
  3. C All visitors on weekends.
    No weekend exception is mentioned.
  4. D Children aged twelve and above.
    Under twelve excludes twelve itself.

איזה שינוי במסיח הפך אותו לשגוי? נסחו תשובה לפני המעבר.

Precision and strength · 11

The new route may reduce travel time when traffic is light. During busy hours, it is often slower.

Which statement preserves the claim?

Evidence & distractor check · 11

Which statement preserves the claim?

Evidence: “may reduce travel time when traffic is light”

  1. A The route can be faster in light traffic.
    This preserves the evidence.
  2. B The route will always be faster.
    May becomes will and the condition disappears.
  3. C The route is never slower.
    The text says it is often slower at busy times.
  4. D The route removes traffic from the city.
    Removing traffic is a different claim.

איזה שינוי במסיח הפך אותו לשגוי? נסחו תשובה לפני המעבר.

Precision and strength · 12

The survey found that most participants preferred online booking, but a few wanted to book by phone.

Which paraphrase is accurate?

Evidence & distractor check · 12

Which paraphrase is accurate?

Evidence: “most participants preferred online booking”

  1. A Online booking was required for participation.
    Preference is not a requirement.
  2. B A majority preferred booking online.
    This preserves the evidence.
  3. C Almost nobody preferred online booking.
    Almost nobody reverses most.
  4. D Everyone rejected phone booking.
    A few still wanted phone booking.

BAGRUT READING WORKSHOP

Cause and effect

סיבה ותוצאה
Ask what caused what. Separate a purpose from an actual result.

Cause and effect · 13

Heavy rain damaged the road, so the bus company changed its route.

Why was the route changed?

Evidence & distractor check · 13

Why was the route changed?

Evidence: “Heavy rain damaged the road, so the bus company changed its route”

  1. A The route change caused heavy rain.
    This reverses cause and effect.
  2. B Passengers demanded cheaper tickets.
    Ticket demands are not mentioned.
  3. C The buses had become smaller.
    Bus size is not mentioned.
  4. D Rain had damaged the road.
    This preserves the evidence.

איזה שינוי במסיח הפך אותו לשגוי? נסחו תשובה לפני המעבר.

Cause and effect · 14

The school opened a quiet room to help students study. A later survey found that many students used it to read.

What was the purpose of opening the room?

Evidence & distractor check · 14

What was the purpose of opening the room?

Evidence: “to help students study”

  1. A To conduct a survey.
    The later survey is not the original purpose.
  2. B To make all students read novels.
    All and novels add unsupported details.
  3. C To reduce the number of teachers.
    Staff reductions are not mentioned.
  4. D To help students study.
    This preserves the evidence.

איזה שינוי במסיח הפך אותו לשגוי? נסחו תשובה לפני המעבר.

Cause and effect · 15

When the factory reduced waste, its disposal costs fell. Managers then used the savings to train workers.

What directly lowered disposal costs?

Evidence & distractor check · 15

What directly lowered disposal costs?

Evidence: “When the factory reduced waste, its disposal costs fell”

  1. A Reducing waste.
    This preserves the evidence.
  2. B Training workers with the savings.
    Training follows the savings.
  3. C Raising workers’ salaries.
    Salary increases are not stated.
  4. D The lower costs created more waste.
    This reverses the direction and changes the result.

Cause and effect · 16

Residents requested better lighting because the path was hard to see at night. The council installed lamps, but has not measured accident rates.

Why did residents request lighting?

Evidence & distractor check · 16

Why did residents request lighting?

Evidence: “because the path was hard to see at night”

  1. A The path was difficult to see at night.
    This preserves the evidence.
  2. B Accident rates had already fallen.
    No accident-rate finding exists.
  3. C New lamps made the path hard to see.
    The visibility problem preceded the lamps.
  4. D The council wanted more night markets.
    Markets are not mentioned.

References

זיהוי אזכורים
Replace the pronoun with its referent and read the sentence again.

References · 17

The teachers gave the students tablets. The students used them to record interviews.

What does “them” refer to?

Evidence & distractor check · 17

What does “them” refer to?

Evidence: “used them to record interviews”

  1. A The students.
    The students are the users.
  2. B The interviews.
    Interviews are what they recorded.
  3. C The tablets.
    This preserves the evidence.
  4. D The teachers.
    Teachers were not used as recording equipment.

איזה שינוי במסיח הפך אותו לשגוי? נסחו תשובה לפני המעבר.

References · 18

The garden attracted birds and insects. These animals helped pollinate the flowers.

What does “These animals” refer to?

Evidence & distractor check · 18

What does “These animals” refer to?

Evidence: “The garden attracted birds and insects. These animals”

  1. A Only birds.
    Only birds leaves out insects.
  2. B Only insects.
    Only insects leaves out birds.
  3. C The flowers.
    Flowers are plants, not the animals described.
  4. D Birds and insects.
    This preserves the evidence.

References · 19

The researchers tested two batteries. The smaller one lasted longer, although it cost more.

What cost more?

Evidence & distractor check · 19

What cost more?

Evidence: “The smaller one lasted longer, although it cost more”

  1. A The researchers’ work.
    The work is not the referent.
  2. B The smaller battery.
    This preserves the evidence.
  3. C The larger battery.
    It continues to refer to the smaller battery.
  4. D The testing equipment.
    No equipment cost is discussed.

What would need a volunteer leader?

Evidence & distractor check · 20

What would need a volunteer leader?

Evidence: “The principal supported the idea, but said it would need a volunteer leader.”

  1. A Every activity at the school.
    Every activity broadens the reference.
  2. B The proposed reading club.
    This preserves the evidence.
  3. C The principal.
    It refers to the idea of a club.
  4. D Maya’s school bag.
    A school bag is not mentioned.

Time and sequence

זמן וסדר
Separate past facts, current facts, plans and conditions.

Time and sequence · 21

The museum first trained volunteers. It then opened the exhibition to the public.

What happened before the exhibition opened?

Evidence & distractor check · 21

What happened before the exhibition opened?

Evidence: “first trained volunteers. It then opened the exhibition”

  1. A The public visited the exhibition.
    Public opening came afterward.
  2. B The exhibition closed permanently.
    A closure is not mentioned.
  3. C Visitors trained the volunteers afterward.
    Afterward changes the order.
  4. D Volunteers received training.
    This preserves the evidence.

Time and sequence · 22

The app currently works offline. Next year, the team plans to add online sharing.

Which feature is already available?

Evidence & distractor check · 22

Which feature is already available?

Evidence: “currently works offline”

  1. A Online sharing.
    Online sharing is a future plan.
  2. B Automatic translation.
    Translation is not mentioned.
  3. C Video calls.
    Video calls are not mentioned.
  4. D Offline use.
    This preserves the evidence.

איזה שינוי במסיח הפך אותו לשגוי? נסחו תשובה לפני המעבר.

Time and sequence · 23

The shop used to close at six. Since September, it has stayed open until eight.

When does the shop close now?

Evidence & distractor check · 23

When does the shop close now?

Evidence: “Since September, it has stayed open until eight”

  1. A At September.
    September is the start date, not the time of day.
  2. B It has stopped closing.
    Staying open later does not mean never closing.
  3. C At eight.
    This preserves the evidence.
  4. D At six.
    Six was the former closing time.

איזה שינוי במסיח הפך אותו לשגוי? נסחו תשובה לפני המעבר.

Time and sequence · 24

If the trial succeeds, the city will expand the service. The trial has just started.

What is the current situation?

Evidence & distractor check · 24

What is the current situation?

Evidence: “If the trial succeeds, the city will expand the service”

  1. A The trial has definitely failed.
    The trial has just started, so failure is not established.
  2. B Expansion will happen regardless of results.
    If makes success a condition.
  3. C Expansion depends on the trial’s success.
    This preserves the evidence.
  4. D The city has already expanded the service.
    A conditional plan is not a completed action.

Complete the meaning

השלמת משפט לפי משמעות
A grammatical ending must also preserve the exact meaning.

Complete the meaning · 25

The library extended its hours because working parents could not visit during the day.

Complete: The library extended its hours so that working parents could ___.

Evidence & distractor check · 25

Complete: The library extended its hours so that working parents could ___.

Evidence: “working parents could not visit during the day”

  1. A work longer at their jobs.
    The purpose concerns visits, not work hours.
  2. B pay more for library membership.
    Membership fees are not mentioned.
  3. C visit outside daytime hours.
    This preserves the evidence.
  4. D avoid reading with their children.
    Grammatical, but the text gives no wish to avoid reading.

The reusable cups cost more at first, but replacing disposable cups reduced spending over time.

Complete: Over time, reusable cups helped the café ___.

Evidence & distractor check · 26

Complete: Over time, reusable cups helped the café ___.

Evidence: “reduced spending over time”

  1. A increase its spending on cups.
    This contradicts reduced spending.
  2. B stop serving hot drinks.
    Drink service is not discussed.
  3. C spend less on cups.
    This preserves the evidence.
  4. D attract every customer in town.
    Attracting customers is not stated.

Complete: Workers were still needed to ___.

Evidence: “workers still handled large packages”

  1. A repair every robot daily.
    Repairs and frequency are not mentioned.
  2. B replace all delivery drivers.
    Drivers are not mentioned.
  3. C handle large packages.
    This preserves the evidence.
