Question & concepts · INTERNAL DRAFT · NOT FOR SUBMISSION

The single question

  • Can “Hittite” in Genesis 23 preserve a normative affiliation with a northern Syrian legal repertoire?
  • Without Hittite sovereignty at Hebron.
  • Without a demonstrated ethnic Hittite population there.

Source: Overview · governing question

Boundary: The question must not be presented as an established answer.

Recall: Does the project need to prove ethnic settlement?

No. It asks whether normative affiliation is possible without demonstrated ethnic settlement.
