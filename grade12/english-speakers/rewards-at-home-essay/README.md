# Rewards for Helping at Home: For and Against

English-only guided essay for Grade 12 English speakers, based on Module G, Winter 2023, Question 10: "Should children be rewarded for helping at home?"

- 32 slides: question and structure, think-first sentence development, four two-sentence paragraphs, full essay, five grammar explanations, grammar summary, connector guide and independent writing.
- Base essay: 130 words. All combinations of the 29 phrase groups produce 130–136 words, before the optional transition sentence.
- Position: ordinary chores are a family responsibility; special effort deserves recognition. The second paragraph acknowledges a counterargument, and the third supports the writer's position.
- Uses `shared/deck/standard.js` for the existing yellow/orange automatic phrase changes, hand demonstration, speed/pause controls and navigation.
- Manual and automatic phrase choices stay synchronized between sentence, paragraph and full-essay slides. Actual visible text is counted, including the optional transition sentence; the full count flags text outside 120–140 words.
- The optional transition appears only in the opening paragraph and full essay. It has three independently changeable segments.
- Touch navigation records the starting scroll boundary. Draft writing is saved in local storage under this page's path.
- The one-sided companion reuses this folder's `build.js`, `interactions.js` and `essay.css` with separate content.

Connector examples draw on the uploaded "Connectors - English with Tamar" handout. Short, natural phrasing was selected and adapted; unsupported claims about research were not copied.

Validation: JavaScript syntax, all local script/style/font paths, exact variant word-count bounds, English-only visible text/tooltips/labels, phrase synchronization, automatic changes, both speeds, pause/resume, hand demonstrations, optional sentence and counts, navigation, reading-scroll boundaries and independent draft storage passed DOM checks.
