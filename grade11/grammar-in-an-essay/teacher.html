Topic

Should every student have a chance to take part in class sports games, regardless of ability?

חיבור לדוגמה: 128 מילים

School sports often raise questions about ability and participation in class games. I claim that every student should have a chance to participate, regardless of ability.

On the one hand, including beginners may reduce a team’s chances of winning competitive matches. If teams include inexperienced players, they will sometimes lose matches because of simple mistakes. For example, repeated mistakes can frustrate teammates who hope to win.

On the other hand, playing together can improve confidence and develop useful social skills. Some students have felt excluded, so joining a team can be especially beneficial. Moreover, sharing responsibility can assist quieter classmates and build friendships.

In conclusion, participation has significant educational value despite the challenges it creates. Therefore, class games should be organized fairly to give everyone a meaningful role.
