The writing task

Should AI tools be used in schools?

Discuss the advantages and disadvantages and give your opinion.

משימת תרגול בסגנון בגרות; נכתוב חיבור בן ארבע פסקאות.

ארבע פסקאות

Opening + opinion → Against → For → Conclusion

מציגים עמדה, מכירים בחיסרון, מפתחים יתרון ומסכמים.

טכניקת ההרחבה

A clear idea + a useful detail

מוסיפים סיבה, דוגמה, תוצאה או תנאי. לא מוסיפים מילים שאינן מוסיפות משמעות.

איך מעריכים משפט?

Clear? Relevant? Explained? Correct?

האם ברור מה הטענה? האם המשפט קשור לנושא? האם הוא מסביר משהו? האם הדקדוק תקין? אורך לבדו אינו שיפור.

Full essay · Conditional + Perfect

AI is becoming common in schools around the world, but people disagree about its use. I claim that AI can support learning in many subjects when students use it responsibly.

On the one hand, students may depend on AI instead of thinking independently. If they copy its answers, they will learn less since they avoid practicing. For example, they may submit incorrect work without checking it.

On the other hand, AI can explain difficult ideas in simple words. Technology has already helped students, and AI may offer further support. Moreover, it can provide extra practice that matches each learner’s needs at home and in class.

In conclusion, schools should use AI carefully while protecting independent thinking. Therefore, its use should be guided and checked by teachers.
