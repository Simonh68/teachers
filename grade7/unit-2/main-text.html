One World, Different School Years

A new school year does not start in the same season everywhere. Summer holidays have different lengths, too. Let us look around the world.

Japan

In Japan, school starts in spring, in early April. The year ends the next spring, in late March. The summer holiday lasts about six weeks. It comes during the school year, from late July to the end of August.

ביפן הלימודים מתחילים באביב, בתחילת אפריל. השנה מסתיימת באביב הבא, בסוף מרץ. חופשת הקיץ נמשכת בערך שישה שבועות. היא חלה במהלך שנת הלימודים, מסוף יולי עד סוף אוגוסט.

Two Places in the USA

Even in the USA, states have different rules and schools have different dates. In Anchorage, Alaska, school usually starts in late summer, in August. It ends in late spring, in May. The summer holiday lasts about eleven weeks. In Miami-Dade, Florida, school also starts in late summer, around the middle of August. It ends in early summer, in early June. The summer holiday is about ten weeks long.

England

In Kent, England, the year usually starts in early autumn, around September 1. It ends in summer, in late July. The summer holiday lasts about six weeks. Dates can change from school to school.

The United Arab Emirates

In UAE public schools, the year usually starts in late summer, near the end of August. It ends in summer, around July 1. The summer holiday lasts about eight weeks.

Jersey: A Small Island

In Jersey, the year usually starts in early autumn, in early September. It ends in summer, in July. The summer holiday lasts about seven weeks.

How Long Is a School Year?

In Anchorage, the school year runs for about nine months. In Kent, it runs for about eleven months. Both include short holidays. In Japan, the year goes from one spring to the next, with holidays along the way.

Calendar details & sources

These are general patterns, not fixed dates for every year. Holiday lengths include weekends. The school-year span includes shorter holidays, not continuous lessons. Seasons use the Northern Hemisphere calendar. Local dates and teacher-training days can vary.
