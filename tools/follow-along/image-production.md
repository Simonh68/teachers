## Prompt set

Shared brief: photorealistic editorial images for a Grade 7 English story;
natural light and fabric textures, modest long-sleeve shirts and trousers,
no animation/cartoon/3D style, no text, logos, brands or watermarks. Keep the
same fictional 12-year-old boys across images. Dan wears a light blue shirt
and a blue backpack; Ben wears a green shirt with a green backpack; Noam
wears a cream shirt and a red baseball cap.

1. Departure: Dan alone leaving a residential building early in the morning,
   holding the strap of his heavy blue schoolbag and looking toward his journey.
2. Journey: Dan near a bus stop at a train station; Ben and Noam stand apart
   nearby without talking; a bus and stationary train establish the route.
3. Winter: Dan looking worriedly at a clear sky at the station, holding a phone
   without calling; no actual rain, because rain is only a future concern.
4. Classroom: Dan looks through an open classroom doorway and recognizes Ben
   with the green bag and Noam with the red cap sitting inside.
5. Friends: the same three boys talking warmly during a classroom break,
   Dan relieved and smiling, his phone put away.

Final edit applied to all five photos: preserve the main boys' identity,
poses, bags, clothes, lighting and photographic style. Dan's headwear must be
an unmistakable navy tembel/bucket sunhat with a soft brim all the way around,
without a separate projecting front visor: not a baseball, flat or newsboy cap.
Ben wears a small beige knitted kippah on the crown, with hair visible around it,
without any brim or visor. Noam keeps the red baseball cap mentioned in the story.
Remove other background people. No other edits.

The rejected intermediate versions (all baseball caps, then flat cap) are not
used by the reader. The final five assets were visually inspected before use.
