# The Road Not Taken — תסריט למורה

48 שקפים · כיתה י׳ · A2–B1 · 20 שאלות ושקפי תשובה נפרדים.

סדר האפשרויות מתחלף אקראית בכל פתיחה ונשמר בזוג השאלה והתשובה. אין להסתמך על אות תשובה קבועה. הבגרות דורשת ניסוח עצמאי; הבחירה הכיתתית היא שלב בדרך אליו. כל השאלות מקוריות לתרגול, ואינן מובאות מבחינה רשמית.

תשובות פתוחות עשויות לקבל ניסוחים ופרשנויות אחרים, בתנאי שהן רלוונטיות ומבוססות. בשאלות דעה הבדיקה עוסקת בנימוק ובקשר לשיר, לא בדעה אחת נכונה.

מקור טקסט השיר: https://poets.org/poem/road-not-taken

תאריך יצירה: יום ראשון, ל׳ בתשרי תשפ״ז (11.10.2026).

## שאלה 1
Where is the speaker, and what must he do?

אפשרות מבוססת: He is in a wood where two roads divide, and he must choose one.
אפשרות חלשה: He is at the end of a journey, and he must explain which road was best.

תשובה לדוגמה: The speaker is in a yellow wood where two roads divide. He must choose one road because he cannot travel both at the same time. This situation creates the central problem of the poem.

הבחינו בין סצנת הבחירה לבין הסיפור העתידי עליה.

## שאלה 2
Why is the speaker sorry at the start?

אפשרות מבוססת: He is sorry because he cannot travel both roads and remain one traveler.
אפשרות חלשה: He is sorry because he already knows that the road he chooses will be wrong.

תשובה לדוגמה: The speaker is sorry because he cannot travel both roads. He wants to explore both possibilities, but he must choose one. He does not yet know where either road will lead.

אין הוכחה שהבחירה שגויה; sorry כאן נוגע למגבלת הבחירה.

## שאלה 3
Why does he look down one road for a long time?

אפשרות מבוססת: He tries to learn about the road before deciding, but he cannot see its whole course.
אפשרות חלשה: He can see the whole road, so he knows exactly what will happen after his choice.

תשובה לדוגמה: He looks down one road because he wants to know more before choosing. However, the road bends, so he cannot see all of it. This suggests that we cannot know every result of a life choice.

ראיה פיזית → משמעות סמלית; לא ידיעה מלאה של העתיד.

## שאלה 4
How does the second road first seem to him?

אפשרות מבוססת: It seems just as fair, and perhaps more attractive because it is grassy.
אפשרות חלשה: It seems clearly better, and he is certain that no traveler has used it.

תשובה לדוגמה: The second road seems just as fair as the first. It may have a better claim because it is grassy. However, the speaker then says that both roads were worn about the same.

perhaps מביעה חוסר ודאות; בהמשך הוא מסייג את הרושם הראשוני.

## שאלה 5
How were the roads actually worn?

אפשרות מבוססת: Travelers had worn the two roads really about the same.
אפשרות חלשה: Many travelers had worn one road, but almost nobody had used the other.

תשובה לדוגמה: The roads were worn about the same. Although the speaker first thinks one may be less used, he corrects this idea. Therefore, the poem does not show a clear difference in popularity at the moment of choice.

זו ראיה מרכזית. אין להסתפק בביטוי less traveled מן הסיום.

## שאלה 6
What does stanza 3 say about the leaves?

אפשרות מבוססת: Both roads had fresh leaves that no step had turned black that morning.
אפשרות חלשה: Only the chosen road had fresh leaves, proving that it was less traveled.

תשובה לדוגמה: Both roads lay in fresh leaves that morning. No step had turned the leaves black. This detail supports the idea that the roads looked similar when the speaker made his choice.

both ו-equally הן מילות ראיה חשובות.

## שאלה 7
Will the speaker definitely return to the first road?

אפשרות מבוססת: No. He hopes to save it for another day, but he doubts he will return.
אפשרות חלשה: Yes. He saves it for another day, so the poem promises that he will return.

תשובה לדוגמה: The speaker says he keeps the first road for another day. However, he doubts that he will return because one way leads to another. In life, one choice may lead to more choices and make it hard to go back.

התקווה אינה הבטחה; doubted מסייג את החזרה.

## שאלה 8
What do the two roads symbolize?

אפשרות מבוססת: They symbolize different choices or possible directions in life.
אפשרות חלשה: They symbolize a clear choice between a good action and a bad action.

תשובה לדוגמה: The roads symbolize choices in life. Like a traveler, a person must sometimes choose one direction without knowing every result. The poem does not label one road good and the other bad.

סמל אינו בהכרח טוב מול רע; שני המסלולים מתוארים כהוגנים.

## שאלה 9
What does the bend in the road help us understand?

אפשרות מבוססת: We can look ahead, but we cannot see everything that a choice will bring.
אפשרות חלשה: If we look carefully enough, we can know every result before choosing.

תשובה לדוגמה: The bend stops the speaker from seeing the full road. Similarly, we cannot see all the results of a life choice. Therefore, the image helps us understand the uncertainty involved in making decisions.

קשרו פרט מוחשי לרעיון; אין להבטיח חיזוי.

## שאלה 10
What happens in the last stanza?

אפשרות מבוססת: The speaker imagines how he will describe his choice far in the future.
אפשרות חלשה: The speaker reports a later event that has already proved his choice was successful.

תשובה לדוגמה: In the last stanza, the speaker imagines telling this story far in the future. He says he will describe one road as less traveled. This is an imagined future account, rather than proof of what has already happened.

I shall be telling הוא עתיד מדומיין, לא דיווח על הצלחה שהתרחשה.

## שאלה 11
What can the “sigh” mean?

אפשרות מבוססת: It may suggest regret, relief, or mixed feelings; the poem leaves it open.
אפשרות חלשה: It proves that the speaker regrets his choice and wishes to change it.

תשובה לדוגמה: The sigh may suggest regret, relief, or mixed feelings. The poem does not give one certain meaning. I think it may show regret because the speaker still thinks about the road he did not take, but another reading is possible.

אפשר להציע פרשנות, אך לסמן אותה כדעה ולבסס. אין משמעות מוכרחת אחת.

## שאלה 12
Does “all the difference” clearly mean a better life?

אפשרות מבוססת: No. It suggests an important effect, but the poem does not say whether it is good or bad.
אפשרות חלשה: Yes. It means the speaker became successful because he chose the less popular path.

תשובה לדוגמה: No, the phrase does not clearly mean a better life. It suggests that the choice mattered. However, the speaker does not tell us whether the difference was good or bad, so we should not add a result the poem does not give.

difference אינו שקול ל-success. הימנעו מהבטחה להצלחה.

## שאלה 13
Choose the linking word: The speaker waits ___ he wants to think before choosing.

אפשרות מבוססת: because
אפשרות חלשה: although

תשובה לדוגמה: The speaker waits because he wants to think before choosing. “Because” introduces his reason. This sentence explains his behavior instead of only describing it.

because = סיבה. הקריאו עם שני חלקי המשפט.

## שאלה 14
Choose the linking word: ___ he hopes to return, he doubts he will come back.

אפשרות מבוססת: Although
אפשרות חלשה: Therefore

תשובה לדוגמה: Although he hopes to return, he doubts he will come back. “Although” introduces a contrast between his hope and his doubt. We do not add “but” to the same sentence.

Although + פסוקית, פסוקית ראשית; לא Although...but.

## שאלה 15
Choose the linking phrase: One way leads to another. ___, returning may be difficult.

אפשרות מבוססת: As a result
אפשרות חלשה: For example

תשובה לדוגמה: One way leads to another. As a result, returning may be difficult. “As a result” introduces a consequence. It connects the speaker’s doubt to the reason behind it.

המשפט השני הוא תוצאה, לא דוגמה.

## שאלה 16
Which answer better explains the title?

אפשרות מבוססת: It points to the road he leaves behind and the possibility he cannot fully explore.
אפשרות חלשה: It points to the road he chooses and proves that it is the only correct path.

תשובה לדוגמה: The title focuses on the road the speaker does not take. It reminds us that every choice leaves another possibility behind. Therefore, the poem is also about what we cannot explore, not only the path we choose.

Not Taken מחזיר תשומת לב לאפשרות שנשארה מאחור.

## שאלה 17
Bridging: “One decision can lead to other decisions.” How does this idea connect to the poem?

אפשרות מבוססת: It connects to “way leads on to way” and explains why the speaker may not return.
אפשרות חלשה: It connects to the two roads and proves that he can explore every possible choice.

תשובה לדוגמה: This idea connects to “way leads on to way.” After the speaker chooses a road, it may lead to more paths. Similarly, one life decision can lead to others. As a result, going back to the first option may become difficult.

משפט נתון לתרגול, ללא ייחוס חיצוני; הדגימו קישור מפורש לראיה.

## שאלה 18
Personal response: What can we learn from the speaker? Which answer is better supported?

אפשרות מבוססת: We can think carefully, while accepting that we cannot know every result of a choice.
אפשרות חלשה: We should always choose the less popular option because it will make us successful.

תשובה לדוגמה: I think we should consider our choices carefully. The speaker looks down a road before deciding, but he cannot see all of it. Similarly, we can gather information while accepting that the future is uncertain.

בחירה בתשובה מבוססת; אפשר לנסח לקח אישי אחר עם ראיה.

## שאלה 19
How does this poem differ from Count That Day Lost?

אפשרות מבוססת: Frost leaves room for doubt; however, Eliot gives a clear standard based on kindness.
אפשרות חלשה: Frost and Eliot both clearly promise success when we choose the less popular option.

תשובה לדוגמה: Both poems explore choices. However, Count That Day Lost gives a clear standard: helping others makes a day well spent. The Road Not Taken leaves more uncertainty about the speaker’s choice and its results.

ציר השוואה: בהירות המסקנה; הוסיפו גם משותף וגם הבדל.

## שאלה 20
Extended answer: How does the poem show uncertainty? Which plan is stronger?

אפשרות מבוססת: Use the bend, the similar roads, and the doubt about returning to explain uncertainty.
אפשרות חלשה: Use the final line alone to show that the speaker knew which road would bring success.

תשובה לדוגמה: The poem shows uncertainty in several ways. First, the speaker cannot see beyond the bend. Also, the roads are worn about the same, so neither is clearly different. Finally, he doubts that he will return. These details show that he must choose without knowing every result.

המודל מצרף שלוש ראיות עם First, Also, Finally. לא חובה להעתיק אותו.
