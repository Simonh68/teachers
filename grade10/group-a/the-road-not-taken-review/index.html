תשובה אפשרית

They can represent different choices in life. The traveler must choose without knowing the full result, just as people do when making important decisions.

A useful tension

Do the roads look clearly different when the speaker chooses?

A useful tension

Do the roads look clearly different when the speaker chooses?

תשובה אפשרית

No. Although one seems to have a “better claim,” he then says they are worn “about the same” and lie equally in leaves.

Think further

What changes between the choice itself and the story he imagines telling later?

Think further

What changes between the choice itself and the story he imagines telling later?

תשובה אפשרית

At the choice, the roads look similar. In his imagined future story, he says he chose the road “less traveled by.” He may be reshaping how he remembers the choice.

Bridging text and context

Robert Frost and Edward Thomas often walked together in England. Thomas sometimes regretted the route they had chosen and imagined that another route would have been better. Frost partly drew on this habit when writing the poem, teasing his friend gently.
