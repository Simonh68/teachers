The Message Without a Voice

הטקסט המקורי נשמר. מספרי המשפטים נוספו לצורך מציאת ראיות.

Part A · Sentences 1–10

1On Monday evening, Noam opened the school website on his family's computer. 2His class was preparing a book sale for a community library. 3He wanted to make friends with Eitan , the new boy, so he asked him to help with the display . 4Eitan's answer appeared a minute later: “Do it yourself.” 5Noam read the three words again. 6They sounded like an order. 7“He thinks he is better than me,” Noam told his brother. 8He felt his face grow hot. 9He started writing an angry answer, then stopped. 10Tomorrow, he decided, he would cancel their work together.

Part B · Sentences 11–24

11The next morning, Noam put the books on the table alone. 12His main concern was the welcome sign. 13The letters were uneven, and the table looked empty. 14When Eitan approached him with a large blue bag, Noam turned away. 15“You don't need to help,” he said. 16“I can see that you don't want to.” 17Eitan put down the bag. 18“ What about the rest of my message?” he asked. 19Noam showed him the screen. 20Eitan looked surprised. 21“The second part is missing ! 22I wrote, ‘Do it yourself for now. 23My little brother needs help. 24I can bring the stand tomorrow.’”

Part C · Sentences 25–36

25From his bag, Eitan took out a wooden stand. 26There was blue paint on his fingers. 27He and his father finished it late the night before. 28Suddenly, Noam understood why Eitan answered so quickly. 29“I assumed you were angry,” Noam said. 30“But I didn't know for sure .” 31“My first words were unfriendly,” Eitan replied. 32“I agree . 33I should explain things more clearly.” 34For a moment, neither boy spoke. 35Then Noam moved the books, and Eitan placed the stand in the middle. 36The welcome sign fitted perfectly .

Part D · Sentences 37–46

37Noam still remembered the hurt he felt. 38A missing sentence did not immediately make that feeling disappear. 39However, the stand was evidence that Eitan cared about their project. 40Noam thanked him and asked about his brother. 41That afternoon, they wrote a short rule for their next project: “If a message sounds strange, ask before you assume .” 42In other words , three words do not always tell the whole story. 43As the first visitors arrived, Eitan stood behind the table. 44Noam moved his own chair beside him. 45“This one is yours,” he said. 46Eitan sat down, and they opened the first box together.
