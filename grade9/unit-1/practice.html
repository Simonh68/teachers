A Clearer Message

טקסט תרגול מקורי חדש וקצר להעברת המיומנות. אינו טקסט נוסף שנקבע לשינון למבחן.

At a school book sale, Dan sent his friend Amir a short message: “Don’t bring the books.” Amir had spent the evening choosing books for their community library. He assumed that Dan did not like his choices. However, he decided to ask before he became angry.

“Do you want to cancel our plan?” Amir wrote. Dan called him immediately. “Not at all,” he said. “The room is locked until ten. Please bring the books after ten, not before.” The second sentence of Dan’s message was missing from Amir’s screen.

When the room opened, the boys worked together. Dan prepared the display, and Amir welcomed the visitors. At first, Amir still felt uncomfortable. Then Dan showed him the full message, and Amir could see what had happened. They agreed on a simple rule: when a message sounds unfriendly, check its meaning before you decide how to reply.

תשובה אפשרית — פתחו אחרי ניסיון

He thought Dan did not like the books he had chosen.

תשובה אפשרית — פתחו אחרי ניסיון

Amir asked what Dan meant before sending an angry reply or canceling their work.

תשובה אפשרית — פתחו אחרי ניסיון

The room was locked until ten. Dan wanted the books later, not a canceled plan.

תשובה אפשרית — פתחו אחרי ניסיון
