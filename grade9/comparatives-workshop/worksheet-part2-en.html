1. Complete the sentences.

1. Tom is as ______ as Ben. (tall)
2. This book is as ______ as the last book. (interesting)
3. The new car isn't as ______ as the old one. (expensive)
4. Her answer is not as ______ as his answer. (good)
5. She speaks as ______ as her teacher. (clearly)

2. Choose the correct answer.

1. The train is (as fast as / as faster as) the bus.
2. This task is (not as difficult as / not as more difficult as) that task.
3. The two bags are (as heavy as / heavier as) each other.
4. The second route isn't (as short as / shorter as) the first.
