1. השלימו את המשפטים בצורת ההשוואה הנכונה.

1. My schoolbag is ______ (heavy) than yours.
2. This road is ______ (wide) than the old road.
3. The new phone is ______ (expensive) than mine.
4. This story is ______ (interesting) than that one.
5. My English is ______ (good) than last year.

2. בחרו את הצורה הנכונה. הקיפו תשובה אחת.

1. This is the (tallest / taller) building in our town.
2. This is the (most beautiful / more beautiful) place here.
3. Of the three routes, this is the (shortest / shorter).
4. This was the (worse / worst) mistake of the day.
5. That was the (better / best) solution of all.
