דף עבודה 2 — השוואת שוויון ואי־שוויון

כיתה ט׳ | שם: __________________________ | תאריך: ______________

1. השלימו את המשפטים במבנה המתאים.

1. Tom is as ______ as Ben. (tall)
2. This story is as ______ as the last story. (interesting)
3. The blue car isn't as ______ as the black car. (expensive)
4. This answer is not as ______ as your previous answer. (good)
5. She speaks as ______ as her teacher. (clearly)

2. בחרו את התשובה הנכונה.

1. This bag is (as heavier as / as heavy as) that bag.
2. The second film isn't (as interesting as / more interesting as) the first.
3. The bus is (not as fast as / not as faster as) the train.
4. This test is (as difficult than / as difficult as) the last one.

דף עבודה 2 — עמוד 2

3. נסחו מחדש את המשפטים בלי לשנות את משמעותם.

1. The bus is slower than the train. (not as ... as)
2. Both phones cost the same. (as ... as)
3. The new book is less interesting than the old one. (not as ... as)

4. פירוק משפט מורכב: סמנו שוויון, אי־שוויון, ניגוד וסיבה.

1. Although the new road is not as short as the old road, it is as safe as the main road because there are fewer cars.
