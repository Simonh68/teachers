# Grade 9 · NYC lesson · updated 01.11.2026
This original text is a classroom summary of NYC history, geography and borough distinctions. It deliberately avoids the false statement that all of NYC used to be called Manhattan.
Official references:
- NYC Landmarks Preservation Commission, Consolidation of the Five-Borough City: https://archaeology.cityofnewyork.us/collection/nyc-timeline/consolidation-of-the-five-borough-city
- NYC DCAS, Important NYC Dates: https://www.nyc.gov/site/dcas/about/green-book-important-nyc-dates.page
- NYC records regarding borough consolidation: https://a860-collectionguides.nyc.gov/repositories/2/resources/74
Materials:
- Printable two-page HTML worksheet: grade9/nyc-five-boroughs/index.html
- Interactive 52-slide vocabulary + grammar review: grade9/nyc-five-boroughs/review/index.html
- Source text: original educational writing. Reading: five paragraphs, seven questions; Present Perfect: three real examples in D and E.
- Teacher-only answer key for comprehension: 1 B; 2 Brooklyn and Queens; 3 examples: Manhattan tall buildings, Central Park, theaters; Brooklyn Coney Island, art, residential blocks; 4 people from many countries have brought languages, foods, traditions; 5 hip-hop developed there/Yankees; 6 Staten Island Ferry, harbor views; 7 C.
- Five-borough illustrated map in review is schematic and not to scale, not a map for navigation.
